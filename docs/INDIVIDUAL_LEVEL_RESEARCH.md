# Development individual-level research delivery

The local research package is under `work/mnq-individual-level-master-research/` (ignored by Git). Start with `MASTER_LEVEL_RESEARCH_REPORT.md`; `MNQ_INDIVIDUAL_LEVEL_RESEARCH.zip` contains reports, aggregate evidence, configurations, scripts and chart examples. Full unsampled columnar artifacts remain local and are indexed by SHA-256 in `EVIDENCE_INDEX.json`.

Ten point-level families were measured over 2020-01-01 inclusive to 2024-01-01 exclusive. PDH, PDL, O5H, O5L, 5m swing highs/lows and 4h swing highs/lows reuse immutable Development study `5cb7fab0d4e7414792a3fbe59ded4999`. PMH/PML use new study `78c96aa0e91147ba9913e365f33ed2f9` and the [official midnight premarket convention](PREMARKET_RESEARCH_CONVENTION.md). There are 176,968 selected event records; overlapping classes are not independent observations.

Supply/demand are explicitly blocked. The unchanged 4h provider never initializes its strict contiguous ATR14 on this extended-session dataset, producing no zones. Its point-boundary research adapter also lacks the requested full zone-mitigation semantics. No replacement detector or relaxed session/ATR convention was invented.

The registered primary family has 672 comparisons at 30 minutes / 1 ATR with one common BH correction. The master report distinguishes numerical primary grading from robustness: 24 of 26 strong-positive pooled associations reverse sign in an exploratory same-date control comparison. Two smaller exploratory observations retain positive effects under both readings, with material adverse-excursion or year-stability caveats. No strategy or profitability conclusion is selected.

All 176,968 point-event records passed causal checks. Chart review covered 147 chronological examples. Independent arithmetic checked 882 source-minute horizon labels (857 complete) and 11,998 ATR threshold/time labels. Independent pivot checks covered 70,081 formations. The relevant engine/API tests passed (72), as did the analysis tests (12). Five key aggregate/report files reproduce byte-identically.

No production engine or strategy behavior changed. No Validation or saved OOS outcomes were queried/revealed. Existing records and source identities were preserved; no paid download was made. Historical 2024–2026 golden runs were deliberately not rerun as part of this Development-only task.

See the [reproducibility kit](../scripts/individual_level_research/README.md) for the exact workflow. Do not mistake the source-study IDs or fixed protocol for a general-purpose parameter search. Future research must use new immutable configurations and preserve this evidence.
