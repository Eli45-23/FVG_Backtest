## Entry and two structural stops

SHORT at the confirmed five-minute downward break candle close; entry fill includes adverse slippage. The candle closes strictly below the fixed level after the prior adjacent close (or current open after reset) was above it. Retain later failures/retests. No next-candle confirmation, indicator, body, time, weekday, month, risk-size or touch filter.

A — LEVEL_RECLAIM: stop one tick (0.25 points) above the broken level. This tests loss of acceptance below that level; a wick through the stop is sufficient.

B — BREAK_CANDLE_HIGH: stop one tick above the entire break candle's high. This tests invalidation of the break candle's upper extreme.

Round stops outward with the validated engine tick convention. Calculate original risk from executed entry to stop; reject and audit nonpositive risk. Do not alter stops because the completed signal candle crossed their future prices. Ownership starts with the one-minute bar beginning at confirmation, not with activity inside the signal candle.

## Payoff and costs

For each stop, exactly two independent fixed-target diagnostics: original 1R and 2R. Engine tick rounding, stop-first same-minute ordering, adverse stop gaps, unchanged stop, no management/partials/breakeven. Exit remaining position at actual session close; the final minute's stop/target takes priority. An event confirmed at session close remains a signal but is NO_POST_ENTRY_SESSION_MINUTES, not a fabricated fill.

One MNQ micro, $2/point. Actual user-supplied fees per side: commission $0.25, exchange $0.35, clearing $0.12, NFA $0.01, total $0.73; round trip $1.46. Primary slippage: one adverse tick at entry and exit. Sensitivity: zero and two ticks each side. Same raw signal population in all scenarios; slippage changes original risk/target mechanically, never signal eligibility.

Every event is evaluated independently. No daily lock or single-position selection is imposed in this research audit. Overlapping diagnostics are not an executable portfolio; aggregate dollar totals and cumulative closed-result drawdowns must be labeled accordingly. Production engine fills are cross-checked independently, with no production code changes.
